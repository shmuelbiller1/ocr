## 1. Purpose & How to Complete Verification

This worksheet pairs each physical handwriting zone on the 300-DPI scan with:
1. The **author/user's initial draft** (preserving original `[?]` / `(??)` uncertainty markers and noting where a line wrap caused an eye-skip in the initial typing),
2. A **provisional 300-DPI visual reading** (offered strictly as a candidate aid for human verification, **never** as settled ground truth where handwriting is ambiguous),
3. **Verbatim raw OCR** (`kraken-ppocrv6-medium` and `mishkefet-v1` greedy) and **optional assisted decoding** (`mishkefet-v1:assisted`, kept separate from raw OCR), and
4. An explicit **Uncertainty Label** and **Verifier Sign-off** slot:
   - `HIGH` — Clear, unambiguous cursive letterforms on clean paper.
   - `MEDIUM` — Readable in context, but cursive ligature, abbreviation tick (`'` vs `"`), or tight spacing warrants human confirmation.
   - `PROVISIONAL [?]` — Overwritten, smudged, superscript/interlinear, eye-skipped in initial draft, or crossed-out in the margin; **must be confirmed by the writer or an independent second reader before any benchmark use**.

Once the original writer and an independent second reader verify every `MEDIUM` and `PROVISIONAL [?]` row (removing all `[?]` markers and recording `verified_by` + consent metadata), the finalized transcript can be placed in `benchmark/modern_school/`.
